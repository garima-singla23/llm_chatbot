Airline: Skytrans Airlines
Review: Brisbane to Toowoomba, and I was amazed at the service provided by this small regional airline, Skytrans Airlines. Even though the flight, in a small prop-jet, was only 30 minutes duration, we were given bottles of water and packets of potato crisps. Returning the next day, service was equally as good. Would recommend this airline.
Rating: 8.0/10
