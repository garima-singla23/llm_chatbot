Airline: Cambodia Airways
Review: Not that much leg room. If you are over 180cm tall, you will be bashing your kneecaps, but otherwise, the seat is pretty comfortable. The services from the cabin crew were friendly and efficient. Due to Covid restrictions, no snack was served on this flight. There is no screen in the seats, so you must supply your own inflight entertainment. Would definitely use the airline again, as their routes are handy and they were punctual.
Rating: 7.0/10
