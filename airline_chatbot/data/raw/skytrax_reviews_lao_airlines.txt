Airline: Lao Airlines
Review: We reserved two seats to fly Lao Airlines non-stop in mid-January 2023 from Chiang Mai, Thailand, to Luang Prabang, Laos. The night before the flight, I logged on to their website to check-in. Surprisingly, I discovered the flight was cancelled. We had never received notice. After multiple requests for a refund, Lao Airlines has promised to refund the cost of the flight, but we still have not received our money back. Additionally, Lao Airlines has refused to pay the incremental cost we incurred to fly another airline above the cost of the Lao Airlines flight. If Lao Airlines had notified us ahead of time, we could have delayed our flight to Luang Prabang by one day or flown one day earlier on Lao Airline flights that were not cancelled. As we did not receive notice, we were forced to purchase a much more expensive one-stop flight on another airline, which included significant luggage fees. Lao Airlines needs to take responsibility for its mistake and reimburse customers for costs incurred due to their mistakes.
Rating: 1.0/10
