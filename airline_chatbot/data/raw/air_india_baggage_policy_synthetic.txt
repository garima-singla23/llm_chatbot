Air India Baggage Policy (Domestic and International)

CABIN BAGGAGE:
- Domestic flights: 7 kg per passenger (1 piece, max 55x35x25 cm)
- International flights: 7 kg per passenger (1 piece)
- Personal item (laptop, handbag) in addition to cabin baggage

CHECKED BAGGAGE:
- Economy domestic: 15 kg included
- Premium Economy domestic: 20 kg included
- Business class domestic: 40 kg included
- Economy international: 23 kg (1 piece) or 32 kg (2 pieces)
- Business class international: 32 kg + 32 kg (2 pieces)

EXCESS BAGGAGE FEES:
- Domestic: Rs 400 per kg for first 5 kg, then Rs 500 per kg
- International: Charged per international pricing

SPECIAL ITEMS:
- Infant baggage: 10 kg included for children under 2 years
- Sports equipment: skiing, golf, diving equipment subject to surcharges
- Musical instruments: should not exceed cabin baggage dimensions

FREQUENT FLYER BENEFITS:
- Flying Returns members get additional baggage allowance (tier dependent)
