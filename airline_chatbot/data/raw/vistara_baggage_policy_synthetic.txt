Vistara Baggage Policy (Domestic and International)

CABIN BAGGAGE:
- Domestic flights: 7 kg per passenger (max 55x40x20 cm)
- International flights: 7 kg per passenger (max 55x40x20 cm)
- One personal item includes: handbag, laptop bag, backpack (max 5 kg)

CHECKED BAGGAGE:
- Economy domestic: 15 kg included (1 piece)
- Premium Economy domestic: 25 kg included (1 piece)
- Business class domestic: 40 kg included (2 pieces)
- Economy international: 23 kg (1 piece) or 32 kg (2 pieces)
- Business class international: 32 kg + 32 kg (2 pieces/multiple), or 69 kg max total

EXCESS BAGGAGE:
- Online pre-booking: Rs 1000 per additional 10 kg
- At airport: Rs 1200 per additional 10 kg

BAGGAGE ALLOWANCE PERKS:
- Club Vistara members: Additional baggage based on tier level
- Infants (under 2 years): 10 kg free baggage allowance

SPECIAL ITEMS POLICIES:
- Sports equipment (golf, skiing, diving): surcharges apply (Rs 1500-2500)
- Musical instruments: must fit within cabin baggage dimensions
- Wheelchairs/mobility aids: complimentary baggage
- Service animals: allowed, no baggage charge

DAMAGED BAGGAGE COMPENSATION:
- Limited to Rs 500 per kg or as per International Air Transport Association (IATA) laws
