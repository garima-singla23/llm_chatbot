Airline: Thomas Cook Airlines Belgium
Review: Brussels to Lanzarote. Nice little airline. Both outbound and return flights on time. Food (premium menu) delicious! Swift and attentive service of the staff! Clean aircraft on both ways! We booked emergency exit seats which offered extra legroom which is a bonus on 4h+ flights. Would recommend this airline for sure, such a shame that this brand will probably disappear.
Rating: 8.0/10

Airline: Thomas Cook Airlines Belgium
Review: Brussels-Tivat. Quick check-in. Friendly staff. Snack and a soft drink for free. Very poor legroom the seat pitch is far too small for most of the passengers lucky this was only a two hour flight. A big minus is the late change of the return flight times (5 days before departure we were informed that the return flight was no longer in the afternoon but early in the morning - reroute of our last days in Montenegro and extra cost for early return of rental car). Also nasty : we paid 50% more for a so-called early booking than for a last minute flight. If you have free dates you better wait as long as possible with this airline.
Rating: 1.0/10
