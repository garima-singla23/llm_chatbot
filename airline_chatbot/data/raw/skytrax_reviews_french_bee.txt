Airline: French Bee
Review: I wasn't quite sure what to expect, having read mixed reviews, but I needed a cost-effective way to move myself, my daughter, and our two small dogs from the US to the UK via France (it's much easier to get a dog into the EU than it is to the UK, and then cross the border from there) and French Bee seemed to fit the bill. Very pleasantly surprised! All the staff we encountered, both at the airport and on board, were extremely pleasant and helpful. The gentleman at Newark who checked us in- two stressed people, two stressed dogs, and a metric ton of luggage.  The flight was packed to the gills with rude people and slightly delayed, but it was the holidays. For the extremely reasonable price (I was able to get two people, two dogs, and six checked bags to Paris from New York for less than $1500, the week before Christmas), the plane was comfortable. This is one of the few flights I have been able to sleep on, so I did not make much use of the entertainment system, but it seemed robust. I did pay for breakfast in the morning, which was nothing over the top special, but not bad and much cheaper than buying something in the airport. The overhead bins were huge and seemed to accommodate a lot more luggage than normal. And for a budget airline, the experience wasn't much different than what you would get from a large legacy carrier, Most importantly, my dogs arrived safe and relatively unbothered by their adventure, though I had serious concerns about them flying in their crates (they are too large to fit in carriers under the seat). Thank you to the staff who handled them so well and kept them safe!
Rating: 8.0/10
