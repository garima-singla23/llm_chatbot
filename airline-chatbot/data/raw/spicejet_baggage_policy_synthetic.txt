SpiceJet Baggage Policy (Domestic and International)

CABIN BAGGAGE:
- All flights: 7 kg per passenger (max 55x40x20 cm)
- One handbag or laptop bag allowed as personal item in addition

CHECKED BAGGAGE:
- Economy domestic: 15 kg included (1 piece)
- Premium domestic: 20 kg included (1 piece)
- Business class domestic: 30 kg included (2 pieces)
- International economy: 23 kg (1 piece)
- International business: 32 kg + 32 kg (2 pieces)

EXCESS BAGGAGE FEES:
- Domestic: Rs 350 per kg
- International: Higher rates apply based on destination

BAGGAGE UPGRADES:
- Additional 10 kg: Rs 399 per flight leg (pre-paid online)
- Additional 20 kg: Rs 699 per flight leg (pre-paid online)
- Add extra bag at check-in: negotiable rates

SPECIAL BAGGAGE:
- Sports equipment and musical instruments: subject to size restrictions
- Wheelchairs and mobility aids: free of charge
- Infant baggage (under 2): 10 kg included

LOST OR DAMAGED BAGGAGE:
- Liability limited to Rs 20 per kg or equivalent international standard
