Airline: SunExpress
Review: Having read the bad reviews of SunExpress, I was not looking forward to flying with them. I’m a very big person, both weight and height, so comfort is not something I get on a plane. The outbound flight was on TC-SOP. This plane was very old and hadn’t been refurbished. It was unbearably hot and the seats were very big, leaving very little leg room. Even my 5 foot 3 mother felt cramped so imagine how cramped I was at 6 foot 3. The flight back was on TC-SOB and this was way better. Plane was modern and had the newer seats. Legroom still wasn’t great for me but it never will be on cheap European airlines. I was on the back row both times which was great because I could recline my seat without anyone complaining, which made the return flight actually very comfortable. I’d recommend getting extra leg room if you are on the taller side (luckily I was travelling with children so I had more room). The service was absolutely brilliant. I contacted SunExpress twice on Twitter about some concerns i had. Both times they replied within half an hour (which is incredible compared to other airlines). The crew on both flights were brilliant too. Very professional and not massively cheesy like some other cheap airlines, but unfortunately some people misinterpret this as them being miserable. Overall, I’d definitely recommend this airline if you are considering flying with them. The only downside was the old plane on the outbound flight, but we were just really unlucky to get that plane.
