Airline: Air Vanuatu
Review: Port Vila to Melbourne. They are in the process of modernising their fleet. Mid 2020, they will be taking delivery of the new A320 aircraft, with longer range new engines and improved efficiency. Air Vanuatu has just launched direct services to Melbourne. It is relieving that no international connection was needed, requiring hopping off the domestic flight then transferring onto the international one, which can be inconvenient. My departure gate was Gate 2, a nice simple process as Bauerfield Airport is small in comparison to major airports. Immigration process was quite swift. I like the small size of the airport, as it is partially open air and you embark the aircraft via the forward and rear stairs. My seat 11A which is a window seat. There are no individual flight entertainment screens, only overhead projector screens, however this was no problem for me, as I had downloaded movies prior to the flight. Dinner was delicious. Extra cudos for having wooden utensils instead of plastic. I chose the peri peri chicken with yellow rice and a a Mrs Higgins raspberry and white choc cookie as a complementary sweet. I then followed up with a couple of white wines which went down nicely. The flight was very smooth  with phenomenal views over the South Pacific.  I loved the cabin mood lighting and the cabin crew uniform which is a lovely light blue. Overall, I had a fantastic flight with no criticisms. I recommend Air Vanuatu and look forward to flying with them again.
Rating: 7.0/10
