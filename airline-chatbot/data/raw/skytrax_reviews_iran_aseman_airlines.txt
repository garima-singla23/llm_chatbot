Airline: Iran Aseman Airlines
Review: I had paid for a flight from Mashhad to Shiraz. When I arrived at the airport the flight time had changed from 2pm to 8pm. I asked if I could change my destination to Tehran (so I could fly to Shiraz from there) and they said yes but insisted I pay the "fine" for changing my flight (a third of the price of the ticket). That's right they delayed the flight for 6 hours and then I still had to pay a fee to change it. Anyway the flight left on the time and included a fairly substantial and tasty meal for such a short flight. The in-flight service was totally unremarkable.
Rating: 3.0/10

Airline: Iran Aseman Airlines
Review: AZD-MHD. Aseman is one of the only airlines that provides domestic flights between Yazd and Mashad. Tickets are cheap and service is ok. There was a delay of 1 hr which feels endless waiting at a small domestic airport. Despite the delay the flight was comfortable on a Fokker 100 aircraft. Not much legroom but fine for a flight of about 90 mins. Some snacks were included and that was ok. Friendly staff.
Rating: 7.0/10
