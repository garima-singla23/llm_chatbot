Airline: Helvetic Airways
Review: LX1579 from Vienna to Zurich operated by Helvetic Airways. The flight was completely full, but boarding was expertly handled by the crew and went very smoothly. The E190-E2 is a small aircraft and space in the overhead bins is limited. The crew assisted passengers with finding a place for their carry-on luggage and in the end, all luggage could be stored somewhere. Also, the crew were very friendly and courteous. As this was a very short flight, a small snack was served, which was excellent. All in all, a very comfortable short flight on a small aircraft with an excellent crew.

Airline: Helvetic Airways
Review: Birmingham to Zurich. I am an incredibly nervous flyer, and was flying at 25 weeks pregnant so much more anxious than usual. When I checked in the airline immediately clocked that I was pregnant and told me to come and queue up with priority customers even though I was flying economy. When I did they showed me to a seat to sit in while I waited for the coach which was so helpful and kind. On the plane the hostess noticed my anxiety during takeoff and throughout the flight they checked in with me constantly with such patience and kindness - offering me a bottle of water to sip and calm my breathing down, giving comforting reassurances at the slightest sign of turbulence, and before we landed coming and offering a little cuddly toy plane for my unborn baby. The genuinely caring nature of all the staff was absolutely superb, and such an enormous comfort to a more vulnerable person. I was so grateful and thoroughly impressed with every staff member involved at each step of the way.
Rating: 10.0/10
