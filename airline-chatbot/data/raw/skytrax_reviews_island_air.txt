Airline: Island Air
Review: I was a little worried after booking Island Air due to some of the reviews I have read about late/cancelled flights. Our flight was on time. I did notice that on days when the weather was very poor that flights were showing some delays, but I guess this would be in common with most airlines flying around the islands. The quick 35 minute hop was pleasurable and stress free. Island Air operates from the 'commuter' terminal at HNL. In reality it is just them and another small airline that shares the facility. No lines at check in, no lines through security. Once airside free wifi available throughout as well as free newspapers and even coffee. Onboard had great views and even some commentary en route by one of the flight attendants pointing out points of interest. Was a nice touch. Served either a bottle of water or a cuplet of juice. Bags (US$15 each to check in) appeared on reclaim belt within a minute of arriving in the baggage hall. All staff in the airport and onboard were really friendly.
