Review: What a surprisingly great budget airline! I wasn't expecting too much because of the exceptionally low airfare. I flew economy without purchasing baggage check in allowance however, I bought an in-flight meal because my flight was scheduled at 4pm and I wanted to have something to do because having dinner in Da Nang after arrival. Seats were a bit cramped in economy class but cushions are generally comfy, which I can tolerable for a 90-minute flight. For people who want extra comfort, it is advised to upgrade to the premium economy class. You'll definitely feel the extra inches of space to accommodate your body. HK Express does offer air ticket promotion from time and time and now the tickets are fairly cheap as the city and Hong Kong Airport reopens international tourists with zero restrictions once again.
