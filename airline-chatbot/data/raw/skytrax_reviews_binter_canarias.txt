Airline: Binter Canarias
Review: Flew from Gran Canaria (Las Palmas) to Tenerife North airport. Flight time was about 20 mins in a small ATR-72 aircraft. Legroom was minimal I'm 6'5" and would not have managed much more than 20mins! Hand-luggage space is limited although your ticket entitles you to 6kg of hand-luggage and a further 20kg of checked-in luggage. The flight time is long enough for cabin crew to serve a cup of water and give you a complimentary biscuit not much but more than most airlines give these days! Flight left 20mins late this was due to air traffic control problems. If you're island-hopping it does the job!
Rating: 8.0/10
