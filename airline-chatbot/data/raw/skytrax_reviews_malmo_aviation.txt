Airline: Malmo Aviation
Review: Malmö to Stockholm. I rebooked on Malmö Aviation due to SAS strike in Sweden. What a sharp contrast to SAS! Check-in was painless using the self-service machines. Flight was operated by an Avro RJ100 with comfortable seats and sufficient legroom. Cabin crew was efficient and smiling. But the nicest touch was the free snack on board: tasty roast beef sandwich orange juice coffee/tea. I was rather impressed by the cabin service on this 1-hour-flight. The only negative point is the free seating policy; as usual it resulted in a free-for-all at boarding. Malmö Aviation is a very nice niche airline and I would strongly recommend it.
Rating: nan/10
