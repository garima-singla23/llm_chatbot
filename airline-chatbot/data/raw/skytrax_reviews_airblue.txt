Airline: airblue
Review: Flew again on Airblue last week, Lahore - Karachi and return. Purchased no luggage ticket that allowed me a hand carry of about 8kg. Check in both ways were quite quick. Followed good Covid SOPs and protocols during flight. Masks were mandatory and they were quite strict with that. Plane was clean with leather seats, middle seats were kept empty unless a family travelling together. Boarding was slightly chaotic but was called zone wise - passengers on last seats were first to board. Hand sanitizers were handed after every 30 min ie twice during the flight. Full Meal service for 1:30 min flights - Light Snacks with soft drink and tea coffee going to Karachi and Full Lunch with soft drinks - tea / coffee returning. Flight lefts exact on time and reached slightly early both times. Disembarking was excellent - strictly 4 person per row were asked to exit while everyone remained seated for their turn. Crew were smart and efficient.
Rating: 9.0/10
