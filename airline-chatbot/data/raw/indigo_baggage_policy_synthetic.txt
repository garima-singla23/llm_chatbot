IndiGo Airline Baggage Policy (Domestic and International)

CABIN BAGGAGE:
- Domestic flights: 7 kg per passenger (1 piece, max 55x35x25 cm)
- International flights: 7 kg per passenger (1 piece)
- Laptop bag or handbag allowed as additional item

CHECKED BAGGAGE:
- Economy domestic: 15 kg included
- Business class domestic: 25 kg included  
- International economy: 20 kg or 23 kg depending on route
- International business: 30 kg included

EXCESS BAGGAGE FEES:
- Pre-paid (online): Rs 350 per kg up to 3 kg, Rs 500 per kg beyond
- At airport: Rs 500 per kg

SPECIAL ITEMS:
- Sports equipment: must be declared at check-in
- Musical instruments: count as cabin baggage if within 7 kg
